Top Notch Inc. Thailand-Japan Business Gateway

01 / Why We Are Here

Thailand has excellent companies. Japan has potential partners.

What is needed is a continuous bridge between them.

Thailand

Many high-quality products, services, technologies, and companies are still not sufficiently known in Japan.

Japan

Japanese companies continue to seek new overseas products, services, technologies, and business partners.

この構想の出発点は、両者が継続的に出会い、情報を届け、関係を育てる仕組みをつくることです。

Top Notch Inc.02

02 / The Challenge

Entering the Japanese market is not only about introduction.

The real challenge is staying visible, understandable, and connected after the first meeting.

1

Visibility

Low recognition in Japan and limited Japanese-language communication.

2

Market Fit

Understanding Japanese business customs, buyer expectations, and category context.

3

Continuity

Maintaining follow-up after exhibitions, introductions, and initial business meetings.

Top Notch Inc.03

Key Insight

A one-time introduction and continuous market presence are different things.

「一度紹介すること」と「市場に継続的に存在すること」は違う。

The platform should help Thai companies remain present in the Japanese market through repeated communication and practical follow-up.

Top Notch Inc.04

03 / Our Idea

Build a Thai Business Showcase on the Top Notch website.

A bilingual platform that continuously introduces Thai companies, products, services, and technologies to the Japanese market.

Introduce

Present Thai companies in a market-ready format.

Communicate

Share information in Japanese and English.

Connect

Create touchpoints with buyers and partners.

Follow Up

Support communication after initial contact.

Improve

Use feedback to refine the platform.

Working name: Thailand Business Gateway / Thai Business Showcase

Business Connection

06 / A Complementary Partnership

This is not a replacement for the Office of Commercial Affairs or DITP.

It is designed as a private-market complement that supports continuous communication and follow-up in Japan.

Office of Commercial Affairs / DITP Thai companies, government support, trade promotion
+
Top Notch Japanese market communication, business network, continuous follow-up
=
More Opportunities For Thai companies in Japan
Top Notch Inc.08

07 / Start Small

Begin with a pilot project, then improve and expand.

Rather than building a large platform from day one, we propose testing the concept with a small number of Thai companies.

Step 1

DITP introduces several companies interested in Japan.

Step 2

Top Notch creates Japan-facing showcase pages.

Step 3

Share information with Japanese buyers and partners.

Step 4

Review reactions, outcomes, and challenges.

Step 5

Improve the model and consider expansion.
