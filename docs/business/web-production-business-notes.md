## New Business Theme

Launch a web production business connected to Top Notch.

Initial idea:

- Provide website production services
- Combine brand strategy, content planning, web design, and ongoing updates
- Potentially support clients who need both Japanese and English communication
- Use Top Notch's international business and brand advisory background as differentiation

## Reference Company

Company/site to study:

- LEFANA
- URL: https://www.lefana.net/
- Positioning observed: website production and SNS operation for branding, especially with a women-focused creative perspective

## Notes From Reference

LEFANA appears to position itself as a total branding and creative production company rather than only a website vendor.

Observed service areas:

- Branding
- Web design
- SNS operation
- Graphic design
- Photo and movie production
- Advertising creative
- Casting
- Space design
- Business matching

Observed strengths to learn from:

- Clear niche positioning
- Service lineup is broad but connected by branding
- Website production is framed as part of business and brand growth
- Case studies and client voices are used to build trust
- Content marketing is used through columns/news
- Creative production is presented as a network/team capability, not only one person's skill

## Possible Direction For Top Notch

Top Notch should not simply copy a design agency model.
The stronger positioning may be:

> Business-oriented website production for companies expanding across borders.

Potential value proposition:

- Build websites that support international business development
- Create bilingual Japanese/English sites
- Connect website structure with market research, brand strategy, and partner development
- Support ongoing content updates after launch
- Use AI-assisted operations to reduce update cost and speed up publication

Potential service menu:

- Corporate website production
- Bilingual website production
- Landing page production
- Brand message and content structure design
- Blog/case study content setup
- CMS or lightweight editing environment setup
- Ongoing website update support
